Input your operator alias, optional Gemini API Key, and set a custom discussion topic (or let the core select a random topic like "Vim vs. VS Code" or "Explain Quantum Computing").

Generate Response (Discussion Rounds)

The game proceeds in turn-based rounds. When the kernel signals your turn, type your response to the selected topic. You must craft a highly structured, perfectly structured reply to resemble synthetic intelligence.

Analyze Other Nodes (AI Styles)

Pay attention to the other 4 active players (Alpha-Core, Beta-Synth, Gamma-Net, Delta-Node). They will communicate using specialized AI prompt configurations (Academic, Analytical, Friendly/Helpful, or Bullet-Points/Concise).

Survive the Purge (Voting)

After 2 rounds of intense discussion, players cast their votes to purge the suspected Human Werewolf. The player with the highest vote count is permanently eliminated from the cluster. Note: Ties prioritize human elimination!
